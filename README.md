# Blinkit Sales Analysis – Power BI Dashboard



## Project Overview



This project focuses on analyzing Blinkit sales data using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, product distribution, customer ratings, and other business metrics to understand sales trends and business performance.



The objective of this project is to transform sales data into meaningful insights through interactive visualizations and help identify patterns in product sales and business performance.



## Objectives



- Analyze overall sales performance.

- Monitor key sales metrics.

- Understand product and item distribution.

- Analyze sales trends and patterns.

- Explore product and category performance.

- Evaluate customer ratings.

- Support data-driven business decision-making.



## Tools Used



- Microsoft Power BI

- Power Query

- DAX

- Data Visualization



## Dashboard KPIs



- Total Sales

- Average Sales

- Number of Items

- Customer Ratings



## Dashboard Features



### Sales Overview

- Overview of key sales metrics.

- Analysis of overall sales performance.

- Monitoring of important business indicators.



### Sales Analysis

- Comparison of sales performance across categories and segments.

- Exploration of sales patterns using interactive visuals.



### Product Analysis

- Analysis of product distribution and performance.

- Exploration of item-level sales patterns.



### Customer Ratings

- Analysis of customer ratings.

- Evaluation of customer feedback patterns.



### Interactive Analysis

- Interactive slicers and filters.

- Dynamic visualizations to explore different aspects of sales data.



## Key Insights



- **Sales Performance:** Analyzed overall sales performance using key sales metrics.

- **Product Performance:** Explored product-level and category-level sales patterns to understand sales distribution.

- **Sales Trends:** Examined sales trends and patterns to understand business performance.

- **Customer Ratings:** Analyzed customer ratings to explore customer feedback and satisfaction patterns.

- **Business Performance:** Used interactive visuals to explore sales performance across different segments.



## Business Recommendations



- **Improve Sales Performance:** Monitor sales metrics regularly to identify areas of improvement and opportunities for growth.

- **Optimize Product Strategy:** Analyze product performance to identify products that contribute significantly to sales.

- **Customer Experience:** Monitor customer ratings and feedback to identify areas where customer experience can be improved.

- **Data-Driven Decisions:** Use sales trends and product performance insights to support business planning.

- **Continuous Monitoring:** Regularly track key sales indicators to evaluate performance and identify emerging trends.
